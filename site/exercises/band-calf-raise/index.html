Band Calf Raise

Calves
Resistance BandIntermediateCalf / Ankle

Why it matters

The Calf Raise builds strength and size in your calves. As an isolation movement, it targets those muscles directly to build size and balance.

How to do it

  1. Anchor the band securely, check there is no slack at the start, and expect the tension to rise through the range.
  2. Set the balls of your feet on a stable surface with your heels free to drop.
  3. Find a light hold for balance if you need it.
  4. Rise onto the balls of your feet as high as you can.
  5. Pause for a beat at the top.
  6. Lower under control until you feel a full stretch through your calves.

Coaching cues

  • Rise as high onto your toes as possible at the top.
  • Lower under control into a full stretch at the bottom.
  • Pause briefly at the top to remove any bounce.
  • Rise all the way onto your toes and squeeze your calves at the top.
  • Match your effort to the band — its resistance climbs toward the end of the range.

Watch out for

  • Bouncing through short, fast reps.

    Short bounces use the tendon’s spring instead of your calves, so they do less work.

    Instead Pause at the top and control a full stretch at the bottom.

  • Cutting the stretch short at the bottom.

    Skipping the stretch removes the part of the range that builds the most.

    Instead Let your heels drop below the platform for a full stretch.

  • Letting the ankles roll outward as you rise.

    Rolling out sends force through the outside of your foot instead of your calves.

    Instead Drive evenly through your big toe and keep your ankles stacked.

  • Letting the band snap you back through the last part of the range.

    Giving in to the band skips the controlled lowering where much of the work happens.

    Instead Control the return as the band’s tension pulls you back.

The detail

Tempo
Rise with control, pause at the top, then lower slowly.
Breathing
Exhale as you rise, breathe in as you lower.
Range of motion
Move from a deep heel-dropped stretch to a full rise on your toes. Take the calves through its full working range each rep.
Setting up
Anchor the band securely, check there is no slack at the start, and expect the tension to rise through the range.
If you are new to it
Do the movement on flat ground before adding a deficit or load.
Once it is easy
Add a two-count pause in the stretched position for extra range.
