Smith Machine Bench Press

ChestTricepsFront Deltoids
Smith MachineBeginnerHorizontal Push

Why it matters

The Smith Bench Press builds strength and size in your chest, front deltoids and triceps. As a compound movement, it trains several muscles together and carries over to real-world strength and power.

How to do it

  1. Set the safety stops, load the bar evenly, and rotate the bar to unlock it from the hooks before your first rep.
  2. Set your grip evenly and pull your shoulder blades down and together.
  3. Set your feet and create full-body tension before you begin.
  4. Lower the load under control toward the middle of your chest.
  5. Keep your forearms vertical as you lower.
  6. Press back to a full lockout.

Coaching cues

  • Keep the load stacked directly over your elbows.
  • Tuck your elbows to about 45 degrees from your torso.
  • Drive your shoulder blades back and down and hold that position.
  • Think about squeezing your chest to drive the movement, not just pushing with your arms.
  • The bar is locked to the rails, so drive it straight up and down that fixed path.

Watch out for

  • Flaring the elbows straight out to the sides.

    Flared elbows put your shoulders in a weaker, less comfortable pressing position.

    Instead Point your elbows toward your hips as you lower.

  • Bouncing the load off the chest.

    Bouncing skips the hardest part of the press, so you build less pressing strength.

    Instead Control the descent and pause briefly before you press.

  • Losing the shoulder-blade set at the bottom.

    When the blades slip you lose your pressing base and control of the bar.

    Instead Keep your blades pinned back and down throughout the rep.

  • Losing control of the bar against the rails on the way down.

    An uncontrolled bar wastes the lowering phase and rushes the next rep.

    Instead Guide the bar down the rails under control every rep.

The detail

Tempo
Lower for about two counts, then press with intent.
Breathing
Breathe in as you lower, then exhale as you press through the sticking point.
Range of motion
Lower until the load lightly touches or nearly touches your chest, then press to full extension. Take the chest through its full working range each rep.
Setting up
Set the safety stops, load the bar evenly, and rotate the bar to unlock it from the hooks before your first rep.
If you are new to it
Start light and groove the movement before adding load.
Once it is easy
Add a pause on the chest to remove the stretch reflex and build pressing strength.
