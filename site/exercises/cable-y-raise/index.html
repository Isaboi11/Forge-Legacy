Cable Y-Raise

Rear Deltoids
Cable MachineIntermediateShoulder Isolation

Why it matters

The Y-Raise builds strength and size in your rear deltoids. As an isolation movement, it targets those muscles directly to build size and balance.

How to do it

  1. Set the pulley to the right height, attach the handle, and step back to remove the slack before your first rep.
  2. Set a tall torso with a slight bend in your elbows.
  3. Start with the load under control at your sides or front.
  4. Lead with your elbows and raise the load to shoulder height.
  5. Pause briefly at the top.
  6. Lower the load under control to the start.

Coaching cues

  • Lead with your elbows and stop at shoulder height.
  • Keep a soft, fixed bend in your elbows throughout.
  • Lower under control rather than dropping the load.
  • Squeeze the back of your shoulders and pull your elbows back and apart.
  • Keep steady tension on the cable through the whole range, including the return.

Watch out for

  • Swinging the load up with the torso.

    Body swing does the lifting, so your shoulders get little out of it.

    Instead Keep your torso still and raise the load with your shoulders, not momentum.

  • Raising the load well above shoulder height and shrugging.

    Going too high brings your traps in and takes the work off the muscle you want.

    Instead Stop at shoulder height and keep your traps relaxed.

  • Letting the wrists roll or drop under the load.

    Bent wrists leak force and move the effort away from the muscle you want.

    Instead Keep your wrists flat and level with your forearms throughout.

  • Letting the cable yank your arm back at the end of the rep.

    Giving in to the cable skips the lowering phase, cutting the work in half.

    Instead Control the return against the cable tension rather than giving in to it.

The detail

Tempo
Raise with control and lower for about two counts.
Breathing
Exhale as you raise, breathe in as you lower.
Range of motion
Raise to shoulder height and lower to a light stretch at the start. Take the rear deltoids through its full working range each rep.
Setting up
Set the pulley to the right height, attach the handle, and step back to remove the slack before your first rep.
If you are new to it
Use a light load and stop the moment your form breaks down.
Once it is easy
Add a pause at shoulder height before lowering.
