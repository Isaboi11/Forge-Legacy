Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press

Front DeltoidsTricepsChest
DumbbellBeginnerVertical Push

Why it matters

The Shoulder Press builds strength and size in your front deltoids, chest and triceps. As a compound movement, it trains several muscles together and carries over to real-world strength and power.

How to do it

  1. Choose a pair you can control for every planned rep and get them into position cleanly.
  2. Sit tall with your back supported and both feet flat on the floor.
  3. Set your grip just outside shoulder-width with the load at shoulder height.
  4. Squeeze your glutes and take a breath to keep your ribs down.
  5. Press the load straight overhead, moving your head back slightly to clear the path.
  6. Once the load passes your forehead, move your head back through and finish overhead.
  7. Lower under control back to shoulder height.

Coaching cues

  • Sitting takes the legs out of it — keep your torso still and let the target muscle work.
  • Finish with the load stacked over the middle of your foot.
  • Squeeze your glutes to stop your lower back from arching.
  • Keep your forearms vertical throughout the press.
  • Keep the effort on the front of your shoulders through the press.

Watch out for

  • Rocking the torso to start the rep.

    This takes work off the target muscle and reduces what you get from the set.

    Instead Keep your back against the pad and move only the working joint.

  • Arching the lower back to press the load up.

    Arching turns a shoulder press into a decline press and takes tension off your shoulders.

    Instead Squeeze your glutes and keep your ribs pulled down.

  • Pressing the load forward instead of straight up.

    A forward press is a weaker, longer path and stresses your shoulders more.

    Instead Move your head back to let the load travel in a straight line over your shoulders.

  • Stopping short of a full overhead lockout.

    Without full lockout you miss the top of the press and the strength it builds.

    Instead Finish each rep with your elbows locked and the load over your ears.

The detail

Tempo
Press with intent and lower under control for about two counts.
Breathing
Breathe in and brace at shoulder height, press, then exhale at the top.
Range of motion
Lower to shoulder height and press to a full overhead lockout each rep. Take the front deltoids through its full working range each rep.
Setting up
Choose a pair you can control for every planned rep and get them into position cleanly.
If you are new to it
Use a lighter load and press one arm at a time if bracing is difficult.
Once it is easy
Pause at shoulder height to remove momentum between reps.

Safety

  • Reduce the load if you lose control of the movement.
  • Stop the set if you feel sharp pain.
  • Use a spotter or set safety pins when the load is heavy on this overhead press, so you can bail a failed rep safely.
