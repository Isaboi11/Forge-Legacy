Glute Bridge Iso Hold

GlutesErector SpinaeRectus AbdominisAdductors
BodyweightBeginnerHinge / Hip Dominant

Why it matters

The Glute Bridge Iso Hold builds strength and size in your glutes, adductors, erector spinae and rectus abdominis. As a compound movement, it trains several muscles together and carries over to real-world strength and power.

How to do it

  1. Set your upper back or shoulders as the pivot and your feet flat, about hip-width and close to your hips.
  2. Tuck your ribs down and brace before the first rep.
  3. Drive through your heels to lift your hips until your body forms a straight line from shoulders to knees.
  4. Squeeze your glutes hard at the top for a beat.
  5. Lower your hips under control back toward the floor.
  6. Hold the position without moving for the prescribed time.

Coaching cues

  • Keep breathing through the hold rather than locking your breath down.
  • Drive through your heels, not your toes.
  • Finish each rep by squeezing your glutes at the top, not by arching your lower back.
  • Keep your ribs down and your chin slightly tucked so the work stays in your glutes.
  • Squeeze your glutes hard at the top of each rep.

Watch out for

  • Letting position degrade as the hold gets hard.

    This takes work off the target muscle and reduces what you get from the set.

    Instead End the hold when your position breaks, not when the clock does.

  • Arching the lower back to push the hips higher.

    Extra height from your spine, not your hips, means your glutes do less of the work.

    Instead Stop at a straight line from shoulders to knees and squeeze your glutes.

  • Pushing through the toes so the heels lift.

    On your toes your glutes do less and the hip drive weakens.

    Instead Keep your whole foot down and drive through your heels.

  • Rushing reps without a real glute squeeze at the top.

    Skipping the squeeze leaves the strongest part of the contraction untrained.

    Instead Pause and squeeze your glutes hard at the top of each rep.

The detail

Tempo
Drive up with intent and lower under control for about two counts.
Breathing
Exhale as you drive your hips up, breathe in as you lower.
Range of motion
Lift until your hips reach a straight shoulder-to-knee line, then lower under control. Take the glutes through its full working range each rep.
If you are new to it
Start with bodyweight and learn to feel your glutes doing the work before adding resistance.
Once it is easy
Add a pause at the top or increase the resistance once you own the movement.
