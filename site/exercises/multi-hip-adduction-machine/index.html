Multi-Hip Adduction Machine

Adductors
Selectorized MachineBeginnerHip Isolation

Why it matters

The Multi-Hip Adduction builds strength and size in your adductors. As an isolation movement, it targets those muscles directly to build size and balance.

How to do it

  1. Adjust the seat and any pads so the working joint lines up with the machine’s pivot, set the pin to your working weight, and keep contact with the support pad throughout.
  2. Set your working hip in position with your torso stable.
  3. Start from a controlled position without momentum.
  4. Move the working leg through its range while keeping your torso still.
  5. Squeeze the working glute at the end of each rep.
  6. Lower the leg under control back to the start without resting.

Coaching cues

  • Squeeze the working glute at the top of each rep for a beat.
  • Keep your torso still and move only at the hip.
  • Control the return rather than letting the leg drop.
  • Squeeze your inner thighs together through the working range.
  • Follow the machine’s guided lever path smoothly and control the weight back to the stack.

Watch out for

  • Twisting the torso to swing the leg higher.

    Twisting fakes extra range and takes the work off the working hip.

    Instead Keep your hips and torso square and move only at the working hip.

  • Rushing reps and losing the glute contraction.

    Without a squeeze at the top your glutes do far less of the work.

    Instead Slow down and pause to feel the working glute at the top.

  • Arching the lower back to gain more range.

    The extra range comes from your spine, not the target muscle, so it adds little.

    Instead Keep your lower back flat and move only as far as the hip allows.

  • Letting the weight stack slam down at the bottom.

    A slammed stack means gravity did the lowering, not your muscles.

    Instead Control the return so the plates settle without banging.

The detail

Tempo
Move with control and pause at peak contraction.
Breathing
Exhale as you contract, breathe in as you return.
Range of motion
Work through the range where you feel the glute working, without your back compensating. Take the adductors through its full working range each rep.
Setting up
Adjust the seat and any pads so the working joint lines up with the machine’s pivot, set the pin to your working weight, and keep contact with the support pad throughout.
If you are new to it
Use no load or a light band until you feel the glute working.
Once it is easy
Add a pause and a band for constant tension through the range.
