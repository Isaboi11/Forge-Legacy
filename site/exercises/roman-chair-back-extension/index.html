Roman Chair Back Extension

Erector Spinae
Selectorized MachineIntermediateHinge / Hip Dominant

Why it matters

The Roman Chair Back Extension builds strength and size in your erector spinae. As a compound movement, it trains several muscles together and carries over to real-world strength and power.

How to do it

  1. Adjust the seat and any pads so the working joint lines up with the machine’s pivot, set the pin to your working weight, and keep contact with the support pad throughout.
  2. Set your position so your torso can move from a forward bend to a straight line with your body.
  3. Set a flat back and brace your midsection before the first rep.
  4. Raise your torso by extending your hips and lower back until your body forms a straight line.
  5. Squeeze your glutes at the top without arching past a straight line.
  6. Lower under control back to the start.

Coaching cues

  • Move by extending your hips and back, not by swinging.
  • Stop at a straight line — don’t arch past it.
  • Control the lowering phase on the way back down.
  • Brace your spine and lengthen through your back as you extend.
  • Follow the machine’s guided lever path smoothly and control the weight back to the stack.

Watch out for

  • Over-arching and hyperextending at the top.

    Arching past a straight line loads your spine and adds nothing to the movement.

    Instead Stop when your body reaches a straight line and hold briefly.

  • Using momentum to swing the torso up.

    Swinging up uses momentum instead of your back and glutes.

    Instead Raise under control by extending your hips and back.

  • Rounding the back on the way down instead of controlling it.

    A rounded, uncontrolled lower puts your spine in a weak position under load.

    Instead Lower under control and keep your back set.

  • Letting the weight stack slam down at the bottom.

    A slammed stack means gravity did the lowering, not your muscles.

    Instead Control the return so the plates settle without banging.

The detail

Tempo
Raise with control and lower for about two counts.
Breathing
Exhale as you raise your torso, breathe in as you lower.
Range of motion
Move from a comfortable forward bend to a straight-line torso, without arching past it. Take the erector spinae through its full working range each rep.
Setting up
Adjust the seat and any pads so the working joint lines up with the machine’s pivot, set the pin to your working weight, and keep contact with the support pad throughout.
If you are new to it
Start with bodyweight and a short range until you can control the movement.
Once it is easy
Add a pause at the top or a light load once you own the bodyweight movement.
