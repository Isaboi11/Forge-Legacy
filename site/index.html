Training

The logger comes first.

It’s the screen you’ll use most, so it has to be quick between sets and it can’t lose a workout. Forge saves as you go.

Request TestFlight access

Made for between sets

The buttons are big enough to hit with chalk on your hands, and everything you need for the next set is already on screen.

  • The rest timer keeps counting if you switch apps.
  • Lose signal and the workout still saves.

Bring the program you already have

On the Workouts tab, tap Build My Own. Paste the plan in, upload the PDF, or take photos of the printout your coach gave you. No plan yet? Answer a few questions and Forge builds one with you.

Open the app, see today

Today's workout is at the top, with the rest of your week under it. One tap starts it.

Hold to seal the workout

When you finish, press and hold to seal it. The Record then shows your volume, your time under iron, and how each lift compared with last time.

  • Any day can be saved as a template.

Run a program week by week

Build your own or pick one made for your goal. Every week is laid out before you start, so you know what Thursday looks like.

  • Start it now, or add it to Planned.
  • Share a program card, or send someone the whole program.

Dark, or Paper

Forge is dark by default. It’s easier on the eyes at 5 a.m. and in a dim gym. Paper mode is a light version of every screen.

  • Switch in Settings.

Nutrition

Track food in the app you lift in.

The home screen has one ring for calories and three for protein, carbs and fat, with today’s meals underneath. Log from search, a barcode, a photo of the label, or your meal plan.

Request TestFlight access

Set targets for your goal

Pick building, maintaining or cutting and Forge works out calories and macros from there. When your training changes, change them.

  • Edit them any time in Nutrition settings.

No barcode match? Photograph the label.

Take a photo of the Nutrition Facts panel and Forge fills in the serving size, calories and macros, plus the small lines like fiber, sodium and vitamins. You add a name and save it.

  • If it isn’t sure about a number, it marks that one so you can check it.
  • The food goes into My Foods for next time.

Coach Holt

Ask Holt what to do today.

Coach Holt reads what you’ve logged: the sets, the lifts that stalled, the weeks you missed. Then it tells you what it would do next. Nothing in your program changes unless you say so.

Request TestFlight access

Pick where to start

Open Holt and choose: build a program, ask what to train today, or change the one you’re running.

  • Or just type a question.

It builds from the training you actually do

Holt sets your split, volume and exercises from your logs and adjusts them as you go. You see each change before it’s applied, and you can turn any of them down.

  • It points out what stalled before it suggests a fix.
  • It won’t send, post or decide anything for you.

Legacy

Build your story. Forge your Legacy.

Legacy is the long-term side of your training. You name seasons as chapters, earn honors along the way, and hold a rank based on years that never drops.

Request TestFlight access

Name a season of training

A chapter is a stretch of training with a point to it, like "Coming Back From Injury" or "The Year I Got Serious." You name it, and you decide when it ends.

  • Workouts, honors, records, photos and goals go into the chapter you’re in.
  • When you close it, you write down what it meant and it locks.
  • Old chapters can be added to. They can’t be rewritten.

Honors for things you already did

Hit a real milestone, like a 225 bench, and Forge gives you an honor for it. There’s no store, and no way to pay for one.

  • Each one stays in the chapter where you earned it.
Request TestFlight access

Rank doesn’t reset when life gets busy

Streaks punish you for missing a week. Rank is based on years of training, in seven families. Take a year off, come back, and it’s where you left it.

  • Foundation, Builder, Craftsman, Architect, Established, Legend, Legacy.

Every chapter, in order

Closed chapters line up into a timeline of your training, with each record and honor where it happened.

Squads

Squads are small and invite-only.

Your training is private by default, and all of Forge works solo. If you want company, start a squad with the people you already train with.

Request TestFlight access

See each other’s workouts

When someone in your squad finishes a workout, it shows up in your feed. Acknowledge it or leave a comment. Nobody outside the squad sees any of it.

  • Set squad goals and run small competitions.

Starting one is quick

Give it a name, a motto and a crest, then share the invite code.

  • Send the code by link, message or QR code.
  • It doesn’t expire. You can make a new one whenever you want.
