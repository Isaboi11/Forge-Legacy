Common questions

How do I delete my account?

Account Settings → Delete Account, inside the app. It is immediate and it cannot be undone — it removes your training record, photos, chapters and squad membership. If you only want a break, signing out leaves everything intact.

How do I switch between pounds and kilograms?

Account Settings → Preferences. The change applies everywhere in the app at once, including history you have already logged.

My notifications are not arriving.

Two switches have to be on. Check Account Settings → Notifications inside the app, then iOS Settings → Notifications → Forge Legacy. If both are on and messages still are not arriving, write to us — that is a bug and we want it.

Can I train without a signal?

You can log a workout, but the app needs a connection to save it to your account. Tracked runs, walks and rides record your route on the device and upload when you are back in range.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Forge Legacy is iOS-only today. There is no date to promise, and we would rather say that than imply one.

How do I manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple, not by us. Open Account Settings → Subscription in the app, or go to iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Refund requests go through Apple as well — we cannot issue them on Apple's behalf, but if something went wrong, tell us and we will help you sort it out.

What happens to my data?

The full detail is in our Privacy Policy. The short version: we do not sell it, we do not run advertising, we use no third-party tracking, and nothing you mark private is shared.

I found a security problem.

Please report it privately to support@forgelegacy.app rather than posting it publicly, and give us a chance to fix it. We will confirm receipt and tell you when it is resolved.
