Forge Legacy — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 August 2026

Last updated: 11 August 2026

The short version

Forge Legacy keeps a record of your training. That record is yours.

  • We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers. There is no ad network in this app.
  • There are no third-party analytics or tracking tools of any kind — no Google, no Facebook, no ad networks. Nothing follows you from this app to another one.
  • We do keep a simple record of which screens and features get used, in our own database, so we can see what is working and what is not. It never includes anything you wrote or anything you lifted, and you can turn it off in Settings › Privacy.
  • Your workouts, photos and reflections are private by default. Things become visible to other people only when you put them somewhere shared — a squad, a friends feed, a competition.
  • Your location is used only while you are recording a run, walk or ride, and your route never leaves your phone.
  • You can delete your account, and everything in it, from inside the app.

The rest of this document is the detail behind those statements.

2. What we collect

We collect only what the app needs to work. There is no data collected for advertising, profiling, or

resale, because none of those things exist in this product.

Account

  • Email address and a password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and stored hashed — we never see or store your password.

Profile

  • Your name or handle, and optionally a profile photo.
  • Training details you provide during setup: your goals, experience level, available equipment, training schedule, and sex where you choose to give it (used to select appropriate exercise demonstrations and strength standards).

Training records

  • Workouts you log: exercises, sets, weights, repetitions, duration, and rest.
  • Programs you follow or build, and templates you save.
  • Personal records, goals, honors earned, rank, and the chapters of your training history.
  • Cardio sessions: distance, pace, duration, and activity type.
  • Body metrics you choose to log, such as body weight and measurements.

Photos and video

  • Progress photos, workout or accomplishment photos, squad posts, form-check videos, check-in videos, and profile pictures — all only when you add them.
  • Photos are resized on your device before upload. We do not read or retain the original full-resolution file, and we do not extract or store camera metadata such as the location a photo was taken.

Social activity

  • Squads you belong to, friend connections, posts, comments, reactions, competition entries and results, and notifications generated by that activity.

Technical

  • Basic information needed to deliver the service and diagnose failures, such as app version and the type of device. We do not build advertising profiles and we do not use device identifiers for tracking.

Product usage

So we can tell which parts of Forge Legacy are worth building on and which are not being used, the app

records a simple record of how it is used. This stays in our own database and is never sent anywhere else.

What a usage record contains, in full:

  • the name of the action, such as “opened a screen” or “started a workout”
  • which screen it happened on
  • your account id
  • a random session id — a new one each time you open the app. It is not a device identifier and cannot be linked to you outside your account
  • the date and time
  • your app version and whether you are on iOS, Android or the web

What it never contains:

  • anything you wrote — no workout or exercise names, notes, reflections, goals, messages, comments, or search terms
  • anything you lifted — no weights, reps, distances or times
  • no photos or video, and no links to them
  • no location of any kind
  • no advertising identifier, and nothing that could identify your device

How long we keep it. Individual usage records are deleted after 90 days. What remains after that is

anonymous daily totals — counts with no person attached.

You can turn it off. Settings › Privacy › “Help improve Forge”. Turning it off stops collection from

the next time you open the app.

You can see it. These records are readable by your own account and by nobody else’s. Deleting your

account deletes them along with everything else.

3. What we do not collect

Stated plainly, because these are the things people reasonably assume an app is doing:

  • No third-party analytics. No Google Analytics, no Firebase, no Amplitude, Mixpanel, Segment, or similar. None are present in the application. *We do keep our own record of which screens and features get used — described under “Product usage” above. It stays in our database, is never sold or shared, and is never joined to anything outside your account.*
  • No advertising SDKs, no ad identifiers, no cross-app or cross-site tracking.
  • No sale of personal information, under any definition, to anyone.
  • No contacts access. We never read your address book.
  • No background location. See below.
  • No health-app integration. We do not read from Apple Health.

4. Location, specifically

Location deserves its own section because it is the permission people worry about most.

  • Location is requested only when you start recording a run, walk or ride, and only as “while using the app”. We never request or use background location.
  • Coordinates are read on your device to calculate distance and pace. Those coordinates are not transmitted to our servers and not stored in our database. Only the resulting distance, pace and duration are saved with your workout.
  • Your route is never shared with other users, because it is never collected in the first place.
  • You can decline location entirely and still log every cardio session by entering the distance yourself.

5. Who can see your information

  • Private by default. Your workouts, progress photos, body metrics, goals and reflections are visible only to you unless you deliberately share them.
  • Squads. Content you post to a squad is visible to members of that squad.
  • Friends. Content you share to your friends feed is visible to people you have mutually accepted as friends. Friendship requires acceptance by both people.
  • Competitions. If you enter a competition, your results within it are visible to the other participants.
  • Your profile. Your name, handle and profile photo may be visible to people who can find you in the app. You can adjust what is shown in the app’s privacy settings.

Progress photos and body metrics are never posted anywhere automatically. Nothing you log is shared

without an action you take.

6. How your information is stored

  • Your data is stored using Supabase, our hosting and database provider, acting as a processor on our behalf. Their handling of data is governed by their own terms and privacy commitments.
  • Data is encrypted in transit.
  • Access is restricted at the database level so that your records are readable only by your account and by the specific people you have shared them with.
  • Photos and video are held in access-controlled storage, not on a public URL that anyone can guess.

8. Keeping and deleting your information

  • We keep your data for as long as your account exists, because the point of the product is a record that lasts.
  • You can delete your account from inside the app, in Account Settings. Deleting your account removes your personal data, your training records, and your uploaded photos and video.
  • Deletion is permanent. We cannot restore a deleted account.
  • Content you posted into a shared space may remain visible to that group in limited form (for example, that a member left) — but your personal records, photos and profile are removed.

Most of this is available directly in the app. For anything else, contact isaiahaltamirano@gmail.com

10. Children

Forge Legacy is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from

them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.
