Screen-Level Card Compositions

Twelve Tier-3 compositions, each assembled from the bound components — Card, ListRow, ProgressBar, Insignia, TimelineRow, Pill, StatBlock, Avatar and Button. Bronze marks only what the athlete earned; comparison stays behind the Performance Firewall; no likes, feeds, shame, or engagement mechanics anywhere.

ChapterCard

05 · HonorCard (CLA-C28)

HonorCard

Recognition, not a collectible. The badge is the Insignia shell with its deferred-artwork slot — never a Phosphor icon. Only earned honors render: no “X of Y”, no rarity, no denominator.

08 · ExerciseRow (CLA-C31)

ExerciseRow

The ListRow at exercise density (72dp). Three states drive emphasis — Active sits on a bronze-edged Surface; Completed carries a bronze check; Upcoming is de-emphasized. Prescribed sets/reps are muted reference text, never enforced.

10 · AccomplishmentRow (CLA-C35)

AccomplishmentRow

Athlete-declared identity, not a computed stat. A filled bronze star marks a Featured accomplishment (max 3, surfaced on Profile); the rest use a quiet outline. A flat ListRow list — name, date, chapter.
