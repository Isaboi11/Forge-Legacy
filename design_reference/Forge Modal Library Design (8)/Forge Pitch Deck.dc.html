The problem

You train for years. Then it all disappears.

Every brutal set, every early morning, every rep past failure — logged in a note, a spreadsheet, or nothing at all. The work was monumental. What's left of it is a number in a forgotten app. Discipline deserves a monument, not a data dump.

The idea

Discipline becomes legacy.

Forge turns training into a story you author with your body. You build a program, you do the work, and the app forges each session into chapters, honors, and a timeline that grows heavier and more earned every year. It's not a tracker. It's the record of a life spent in the arena.

01 — In the arena

The workout logger

Heavy, fast, and out of your way. Log sets with a thumb, watch rest tick down, and feel the weight of the session build in real time — no fiddling between reps.

◆One-thumb set logging built for mid-lift
◆Rest timer and live session weight
◆Every rep saved the instant you rack it

Why it matters — the effort is captured while it's still hot, so nothing you earn is ever lost.

02 — Author the plan

The program builder

Set your split, your weeks, and your work — repeat a template or shape every week by hand. Pulled from a real exercise library, it's a plan you own instead of one you rent.

◆Choose length, split, and days per week
◆Repeat a week or customize each one
◆Build from a full library of exercises

Why it matters — a legacy has to be yours to mean anything. You design the path you'll be judged by.

03 — The reckoning

The honors ceremony

Finish a session and the work comes alive. Forge stages a moment — the session recounted, honors struck in bronze, your record advanced. Effort ends in ceremony, not a silent save.

◆Earned honors, forged and awarded
◆A reflection captured while it's raw
◆The session written into your chapter

Why it matters — closing the loop with ceremony is what turns a workout into a memory you keep.

04 — The monument

Your legacy hub

Every session flows into one place: chapters that mark your eras, a timeline of what you've done, and insignia that rank the discipline behind it. Open it in year three and feel the weight of the work.

◆Chapters that mark your training eras
◆A living timeline of every milestone
◆Insignia and ranks earned over time

Why it matters — this is the payoff for years of consistency: proof, in one place, that you showed up.

05 — The evidence

Progress & transformation

Watch the lines climb and the body change. Forge tracks your numbers over time and holds your transformation photos side by side, so the slow, invisible work finally becomes something you can see.

◆Strength and volume trends over time
