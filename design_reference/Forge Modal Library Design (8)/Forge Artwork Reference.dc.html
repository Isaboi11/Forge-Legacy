§ 01 · Purpose

Forge Legacy should feel like one handcrafted product.

Every illustration should feel as though it came from the same artist, working in the same material, over many years. The moment a viewer sees any image, it should communicate:

Nothing should ever read as modern, cartoon, comic-book, or overtly digital. If an illustration feels energetic or trendy, it does not belong in the system.

§ 03 · UI Philosophy

Artwork supports. It never competes.

Illustrations exist to add atmosphere and identity. They are the backdrop, not the subject. The interface — its titles, actions, and information — always remains the primary focus of the screen.
