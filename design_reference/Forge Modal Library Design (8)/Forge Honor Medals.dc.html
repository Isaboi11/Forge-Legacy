III The cut

Nothing sits on the disc. Every stroke is cut into it — the foundation’s icon law, made literal. Light falls from above, so each groove gathers shadow along its upper wall while its lower lip catches the light. Three offset passes per layer; this is what separates a struck medal from a vector icon on a bronze circle.

IV Material Rank changes the metal, never the drawing

The same medal at all four tiers. The drawing never changes — the rim does: bare, then struck, then studded, then a twelve-ray corona over the metallic sweep. Structure first, colour second, so magnitude is obvious at 44px without reading a number. This is what keeps a ten-step ladder from becoming ten unrelated drawings.
