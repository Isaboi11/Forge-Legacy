Difference two · the people

A squad is a handful of people, not an audience.

The two things the rest of the category offers are a public feed of strangers' workouts, or a group that punishes you when you miss. Both are engagement mechanics. Neither is what training with people actually feels like.

Difference four · what we're bound by

Four things this app will never do.

Not policies we could quietly change next quarter. Constraints the app is built on.

01 Charge you for your own history. Everything you log is yours, forever. No tier locks your past, and letting a subscription lapse never takes it away.
02 Make you feel bad to keep you coming back. Miss a week and the app says nothing. No "days since your last workout." No red. No guilt notification.
03 Show your training to strangers. No public profiles, no discoverable stats, no feed anyone outside your squad can see. Per-section controls, private by default.
04 Let anyone rewrite what happened. Sealed is sealed. You can add to your legacy; you cannot revise it. That's what makes it worth having.

None of that matters if the training app isn't good.

So it's good. Weight, reps, done — a set logged in under two seconds. A rest timer that keeps running when you leave the app. Autosave, so you can lose signal mid-workout and come back to exactly where you were. Records caught as you lift.

The obvious questions

I already use another app.+

Run both for a couple of weeks. The program builder takes your existing program today, so you're not starting over to try it — and if you leave, nothing you logged here gets held hostage.

Isn't "no comparison" bad for motivation?+

Comparison is rented motivation. It works until the streak breaks or the leaderboard demoralises you, and then the app gets deleted. We're not optimising for day-one usage; we're optimising for whether you still want this in year five.

Is my data private?+

Yes, by default. Per-section visibility controls, no public profiles, and row-level security on every table. Nothing about you is discoverable by a stranger.

What if I stop for a while?+

Nothing happens. Your rank doesn't fall, your chapter doesn't fail, and the app doesn't send you a guilt notification. Come back and keep going.

Which phone do I need?+

iPhone. Forge Legacy is an iOS app. Android isn't ready yet, and we'd rather say so.

Does it track calories?+

No, and it isn't going to. Nutrition is a different product. This one does training and what training builds.

How is it built?+

161 database migrations, security policies on every table, 2,362 automated tests, and a live backend. A real application, not a prototype.
