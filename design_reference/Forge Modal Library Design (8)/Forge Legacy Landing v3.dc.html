What we're bound by

Four promises, in the architecture — not the marketing.

01 Never charge for history. Everything you log is yours, forever. No tier locks your past.
02 No shame mechanics. Miss a week and the app says nothing. No "days since your last workout." No red.
03 Nothing is public. No followers, no public stats, no feed a stranger can see.
04 History can't be rewritten. Sealed is sealed. You can add to your legacy; you cannot revise it.

The obvious questions

Is my data private?+

Yes, by default. Per-section visibility controls, no public profiles, and row-level security on every table. Nothing about you is discoverable by a stranger.

Which phone do I need?+

iPhone. Forge Legacy is an iOS app. Android isn't ready yet, and we'd rather say so than take your email for a list.

I already use another app.+

Run both for a couple of weeks. The program builder takes your existing program today, so you're not starting over to try it.

What if I stop for a while?+

Nothing happens. Your rank doesn't fall, your chapter doesn't fail, and the app doesn't send you a guilt notification. Come back and keep going.

Does it track calories?+

No, and it isn't going to. Nutrition is a different product. This one does training and what training builds.

How is it built?+

161 database migrations, security policies on every table, 2,362 automated tests, and a live backend. A real application, not a prototype.
