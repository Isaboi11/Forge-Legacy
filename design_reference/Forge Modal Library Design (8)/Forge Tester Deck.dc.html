The people

Small. Private. The people you actually train with.

Squads surface what members have done — never what they have missed.

There is a feed — on the friends side and inside every squad. Both hold only people you added. No followers. No strangers.

You can acknowledge a post. It stays on the post — it isn’t public, it isn’t ranked, and it doesn’t become a score anywhere. Nothing posts on its own.

The people

What a squad actually does.

01 Check in, or don’t.Seven kinds of post — a session, a photo, a note, a milestone. Every one of them manual. Nothing posts on your behalf.
02 Squad records.The heaviest lift in the group, held by whoever earned it. A record has a name on it, not a rank.
03 Train together, apart.Invite someone to the same workout. You both log your own sets, on your own schedule.
04 Added by handle only.No contact upload. No suggested friends. Nobody finds you unless you hand them your handle.
The people

A private competition inside your squad. A real finish line. A champion at the end.

Opt in, always. Not joining leaves no trace.
