A training app for those who show up

Forge Legacy

Build your story. Forge your legacy.

The problem

You've trained for years. Almost none of it left a trace.

Every session ends the same way — the work evaporates the moment you rack the weight. No record, no story, nothing to stand on. Discipline that deserves to be permanent disappears into the next day.

The idea

Forge carves every session into a record that lasts.

Log the work, earn the honor, watch the chapters stack up. And you don't do it alone — your squad, your friends, and a little honest accountability keep you showing up.
