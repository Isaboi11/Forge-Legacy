## Section 3 — Family-Level Questions

**A — Did all six programs survive governance?** **Yes — all APPROVE.** Zero retirements.

**B — Are any convergence risks unresolved?** **No.** The two genuine risks are resolved with falsifiable, in-structure proof: Conditioning Intermediate ↔ Body Recomposition Intermediate (resolved — resistance-forward/deficit vs conditioning-forward/work-capacity) and Athletic Conditioning Foundation ↔ Body Recomposition Foundation (resolved — the same inversion at beginner level). Hybrid Foundation and Hybrid Intermediate have no goal+environment peer and were independently validated.

**C — Are any programs weak enough to retire?** **No.** No program rests on catalog topology; each is carried by a distinct, goal-driven training intent. The PEA §2.2 over-cap exception (6 programs, 3 distinct sub-goals) is **validated** by the blueprint set rather than merely asserted.

**D — Are the successor chains coherent?** **Yes.** Three independent terminal-at-INTERMEDIATE ladders, reciprocal and orphan-free. The Athletic Conditioning Foundation → Conditioning Intermediate **naming asymmetry** (mismatched prefixes) is a cosmetic clarity item, not an integrity defect (Section 5).

**E — Are the three ladders meaningfully distinct?**
- **Athletic Conditioning** — work capacity / general fitness; conditioning-forward; General athlete; the only 3-day ladder.
- **Body Recomposition** — fat loss + muscle retention; resistance-forward/deficit; Hybrid athlete; CONDITIONING/HYPERTROPHY boundary.
- **Hybrid** — concurrent strength + conditioning; MIXED environment; interference-managed; Hybrid athlete.
**Yes — distinct on goal signature, training intent, and (for Hybrid) environment.** This is the strongest goal-differentiation of any family reviewed.

**F — Is the Conditioning family ready for Stage 2 authoring?** **Yes, from a governance standpoint.** The same cross-family Stage-2 preconditions apply (Exercise Library population; the pre-publish PAS-R1 Difficulty Calibration Audit) — neither is a Stage-1 defect. One **family-specific Stage-2 caveat:** because two of the distinctions are emphasis-inversions within the shared §11.3 "combine strength + cardio" template, Stage 2 authoring **must preserve the inversion and the work:rest/`restSeconds` discipline**, or the GYM pairs could converge in authored practice (Section 5, watch item 1).

## Section 4 — Evaluation of the Major Stage 1 Finding

**Claim:** the family originally suspected of the **highest** convergence risk (Cross-Family Governance Review: largest ungoverned family, most INTERMEDIATE programs, suspected Powerbuilding-mirror) ultimately proved to be **one of the strongest goal-differentiated families.**

**Is it supported by the blueprint set? — Yes, and strongly, with one honest nuance.**

Supported because: every one of the six programs' distinctions is carried by **genuine goal-driven training content** — work-capacity-forward (Conditioning), resistance-forward/deficit (Recomposition), concurrent strength+conditioning (Hybrid) — demonstrated in authored structure, not catalog topology. Goal alignment, which was *shared and inert* in the Powerbuilding Intermediate retirement (leaving only topology), is here the **live primary differentiator across all three ladders**. The family is the inverse of the retirement case, exactly as Stage 0A predicted.

**The nuance (so the conclusion is not overstated):** "strongly goal-differentiated" does **not** mean "trivially distinct." The two same-environment GYM pairs (Conditioning Int ↔ Body Recomp Int; AC Foundation ↔ Body Recomp Foundation) were near metadata-twins and required **explicit structural proof** — a falsifiable emphasis-inversion test — before they could be declared distinct. The differentiator existed and was live, but it had to be *demonstrated*, not assumed. That is the correct reading: the family is goal-differentiation-strong, and the blueprint set earned that conclusion through testing rather than inheriting it.

## Section 5 — Watch Items (for future reviews / Stage 2)

1. **Emphasis-inversions are load-bearing.** The GYM pairs are distinguished by conditioning-forward vs resistance-forward *authored content* on a shared §11.3 template. Stage 2 must preserve the inversion (and the work:rest/`restSeconds` discipline); if authored sessions blur it, the pairs converge in practice despite distinct goals.
2. **Body Recomposition ladder's CONDITIONING/HYPERTROPHY boundary.** It is resistance-forward yet categorized CONDITIONING (correct — distinguished by LOSE_FAT + conditioning component + deficit). Re-check if the Muscle Building family ever adds a fat-loss/recomp variant.
3. **Athletic Conditioning Foundation's 3-day frequency is a load-bearing distinction axis** (the family's only 3-day program). Do not "harmonize" it to 4 days without re-running the Foundation convergence test — the Lower Body Foundation lesson.
4. **Naming asymmetry** — Athletic Conditioning Foundation → Conditioning Intermediate (mismatched prefixes). Cosmetic; likely part of the open naming/positioning conflict (Production Standard §1). Resolve in a future cosmetic/naming pass, not a blocker.
