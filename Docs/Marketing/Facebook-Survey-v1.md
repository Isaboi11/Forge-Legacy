# Workout App Survey — v1 (Facebook groups)

Created 2026-09-29. **Live form: https://forms.gle/BukyU3tJE6HsXcAP9** (responses go to the "What do you love (and hate) about workout apps? (Responses)" Google Sheet, and — once its setup script is pasted in — to the CRM: `/admin` → Surveys, migration 0243; early-access emails land in Contacts). **Rules for posting:** read each group's rules first (many ban promo). Don't name or pitch Forge Legacy in the post or the survey — people answer more honestly and it won't get deleted as an ad.
