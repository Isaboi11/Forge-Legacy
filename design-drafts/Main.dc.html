Navigation architecture — the decision

Keep the scroll. Add addresses.

The three template references you sent — Spark, smartHome, Leno — all solve a problem you do not have. They sell a feature list, so a nav bar that jumps between features costs them nothing. Forge Legacy sells a sequence: chapters, then sealing, then rank, then the four refusals. Break that into tabs and the argument stops arriving in order.

Rejected
Tabs

Splits a narrative into unordered panels and hides most of the pitch behind a click. Worst of all, it is still one URL — it adds interaction cost and buys zero search surface.

Costs a visitor. Gains nothing.
Rejected
Template multi-page

Home / About / Features / Pricing / Contact, as in the three references. It manufactures five thin pages out of one strong one and scatters the argument across them. Your page already scored 9/10 on design — higher than Hevy or Boostcamp. This is a downgrade.

More URLs, less argument.
Recommended
Sticky anchor nav
+ real content pages

The landing page keeps its scroll and gains wayfinding. New URLs carry the things that were never part of the argument — the catalogue, the guides. Search surface arrives without touching the pitch.

Additive. Nothing gets undone first.

Order of work
1
Give the page something to click TestFlight link, or one email capture. Half a day. Until the listing is live this is the only thing costing you visitors outright.
2
Sticky anchor nav Five anchors and a CTA. No new pages, no new content — it only makes the page you already have navigable.
3
Publish the exercise catalogue The single highest-return item on the list, and a publishing job rather than an authoring one — the content exists.
4
Author robots.txt + sitemap.xml Today's robots.txt is Cloudflare boilerplate with no Sitemap: line. Pointless before step 3; mandatory after it.
One thing not to copy

A page that does not exist stays neutral. A page that exists and is wrong does damage — Hevy's empty reviews page, its self-contradicting status page. As you add addresses, ship none of them half-built.
